Biometric Access Control

Real-time turnstile sync & automatic member lock

✅ Active Polling & Sync
The system polls attendance from the Hikvision terminal every 15 seconds. When a member's plan expires, their biometric door access is automatically revoked until renewed.
🔄 Biometric Auto-Restore
When anyone expires, their name and biometric credentials are removed from the machine and preserved in the local database. When renewed, their biometric credentials automatically reappear at the machine!
Troubleshooting Tip: Ensure this gym PC and the Hikvision device are connected to the same local network router / switch.
